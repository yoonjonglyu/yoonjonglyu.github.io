Privacy Policy for DaoXin

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 | Effective Date: October 26, 2026 (Previous: May 24, 2026)

RyuisLabs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DaoXin mobile application (the "Service") for users worldwide. Your privacy is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle, protect, and process your data in compliance with global privacy regulations, including the GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA/CPRA (USA), COPPA, and PIPA (South Korea).

1. Information Collection and Use (Local-First Architecture)

DaoXin is engineered strictly as a local-first cultivation and habit-tracking tool. We do not operate central user databases, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal task logs, spiritual progress (Qi), sudden realizations (Notes), or overall cultivation statistics to any external servers owned by us. All core data generated within the Service is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.

2. Local Data Export & Import (JSON)

To grant you complete sovereignty over your cultivation data, DaoXin provides a manual data management feature:

  • Data Control & Portability: You can export all your logs, progress, breakthrough ranks, and historical stats into a structured JSON file at any time.
  • Local Execution: The generation, export, and import of this backup file happen entirely on your local device. We have no access to the files you create or transfer through this feature.

3. Google Drive Synchronization (Optional)

DaoXin provides an optional cloud backup and synchronization feature using the Google Drive API to safeguard your progress across devices. This feature is turned off by default and only activates upon your explicit consent and Google Sign-In:

  • Restricted Data Scope: The Service requests permission strictly to the isolated "Application Data Folder" (appDataFolder) created by DaoXin on your Google Drive. We cannot view, access, or modify any other personal files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Encrypted Transmission: Your data is transmitted securely and directly between your device and Google's servers via encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS). No data routes through our infrastructure.
  • Purpose: Utilized solely to synchronize your cultivation journey, breakthrough ranks, and notes across multiple devices or to restore your data upon reinstallation.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party Advertising & International Data Transfers (Google AdMob)

To sustain the continuous development of the ABILITY series and offer our core features for free, we integrate Google AdMob. When serving advertisements or synchronizing cloud backups, certain technical data is processed by Google LLC on its global server infrastructure (including servers located in the United States and other countries outside your jurisdiction):

  • How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:
    • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

5. App Permissions

To ensure optimal functionality and immersion, DaoXin may request the following permissions on your device:

  • Notifications (Optional): Used to deliver timely reminders for your daily cultivation tasks and to notify you of major progress milestones.
  • Storage / File Access (Optional): Used exclusively via the system file picker when you manually export or import JSON backup files.
  • Identity / Accounts (Optional): Used strictly for Google Sign-In to establish a secure connection when you enable the Google Drive synchronization feature.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Retained solely on your device. Uninstalling the Service or clearing the app storage in your device settings permanently deletes all local cultivation logs, notes, and progress immediately.
  • Google Drive Backup Data: Retained in your personal Google Drive until you delete the backup within the app or revoke access and delete application data via your Google Account Permissions.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact us via email, your email address and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry (up to 1 year) and permanently deleted thereafter.

7. Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA & International Laws)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California/USA, South Korea, and Brazil), you have the right to access, rectify, export, restrict, or erase your data, as well as the right to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising:

  • Exercising Your Rights: Because we do not maintain central user accounts on our own servers, you can directly exercise your rights to export data (via JSON export), erase data (by clearing local app storage), or withdraw consent (by revoking Google Drive permissions or resetting your Advertising ID) at any time.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell your personal information for monetary value. You may opt out of third-party ad sharing via the device settings described in Section 4.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

DaoXin is safe for users of all ages. Because all primary data is managed locally on your device, we do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, such as 14 or 16). Advertisements served through Google AdMob adhere strictly to Google's policy requirements regarding families and children. If you believe we have inadvertently received personal information from a child via support channels, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory changes. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at least 7 days prior to taking effect.
