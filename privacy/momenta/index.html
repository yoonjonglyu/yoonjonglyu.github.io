Privacy Policy for ABILITY: Gravity & Time

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 | Effective Date: October 26, 2026 (Previous: April 08, 2026)

RyuisLabs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the ABILITY: Gravity & Time (formerly known as Momenta) mobile application (the "Service") for users worldwide. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, protect, and process your data in compliance with global privacy regulations, including the GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA/CPRA (USA), COPPA, and PIPA (South Korea).

1. Information Collection and Use

ABILITY: Gravity & Time is engineered as a local-first productivity tool. We do not operate central user databases, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal focus logs or usage statistics to our own external servers.

  • Local Device Data: All core content created within the Service (Focus logs, Level progress, and App Settings) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
  • Optional Cloud Backup Data: If you explicitly enable Google Drive Synchronization, your app data is backed up directly to your personal Google Drive account.
  • Third-Party Advertising Data: To support free features, our integrated advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and diagnostic telemetry as detailed in Section 3.

2. Google Drive Synchronization (Optional)

The Service provides an optional cloud backup and synchronization feature using the Google Drive API. This feature is disabled by default and is only activated upon your explicit consent and Google Sign-In:

  • Restricted Data Scope: The Service requests access strictly to the isolated "Application Data Folder" (appDataFolder) created by ABILITY: Gravity & Time on your Google Drive. We cannot view, modify, or access any of your other personal files or folders.
  • Encrypted Transmission: Your focus data is transmitted directly between your device and Google's servers via encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Purpose: Used solely to keep your focus history and progress synchronized across your devices and to provide a user-controlled backup solution.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Third-Party Advertising & International Data Transfers

To provide the "Energy Recharge" feature for free, we use Google AdMob. When serving advertisements or synchronizing backups, data is processed by Google LLC on its global server infrastructure (including servers located in the United States and other countries outside your jurisdiction):

For more details on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

  • How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings:
    • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

4. App Permissions

To ensure full functionality, ABILITY: Gravity & Time may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications (Optional): Used to alert you when a focus session ends or when energy is fully recharged.
  • Identity / Accounts (Optional): Used strictly for Google Sign-In when you choose to activate the Google Drive synchronization feature.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Retained solely on your device. Uninstalling the Service or clearing the app storage in your device settings permanently deletes all local focus logs and progress immediately.
  • Google Drive Backup Data: Retained in your personal Google Drive until you delete the backup within the app or revoke access and delete application data via your Google Account Permissions.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact us via email, your email address and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry (up to 1 year) and permanently deleted thereafter.

6. Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA & International Laws)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California/USA, South Korea, and Brazil), you have the right to access, rectify, export, restrict, or erase your data, as well as the right to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising:

  • Exercising Your Rights: Because we do not maintain central user accounts on our own servers, you can directly exercise your rights to erase data or withdraw consent at any time by clearing your local app storage, revoking Google Drive permissions, or resetting your device's Advertising ID.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell your personal information for monetary value. You may opt out of third-party ad sharing via the device settings described in Section 3.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ABILITY: Gravity & Time is safe for users of all ages. Because core focus data is stored locally on your device, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, such as 14 or 16). Advertisements served via Google AdMob comply with Google's Families and Children policies. If you believe we have inadvertently received personal information from a child via support channels, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory changes. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at least 7 days prior to taking effect.
