Privacy Policy for MemoFlow

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 | Effective Date: October 26, 2026 (Previous: January 08, 2026)

RyuisLabs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the MemoFlow application across mobile, desktop, and web platforms (the "Service") for users worldwide. Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, protect, and process your data in compliance with global privacy regulations, including the GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA/CPRA (USA), COPPA, and PIPA (South Korea).

1. Information Collection and Use (Local-First Architecture)

MemoFlow is engineered strictly as a local-first productivity tool. We do not operate central user databases, and we do not collect, store, access, or transmit any of your personal data, memos, or notes to our own external servers.

  • Local Device Storage: All content created within the Service (Memos, To-Do lists, Drafts, and Settings) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
  • Local Data Export (HTML, Markdown, JSON): You can export your notes into HTML, Markdown, or JSON files at any time. The generation and saving of these files occur entirely on your local device.

2. Google Drive Synchronization (Optional)

MemoFlow provides an optional cloud backup and synchronization feature using the Google Drive API. This feature is disabled by default and is only activated if you explicitly choose to sign in with your Google Account:

  • Restricted Data Access: The Service requests access strictly to the isolated "Application Data Folder" (appDataFolder) or specific sync files created by MemoFlow on your Google Drive. We cannot view, access, or modify any other personal files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Direct Encrypted Transmission: Your encrypted memo data is transmitted directly between your device and Google's global servers (operated by Google LLC, including in the United States) via encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS). No data ever passes through our infrastructure.
  • Purpose: Used solely to keep your notes synchronized across multiple devices (Android, Windows, Web) and to provide a user-controlled backup solution.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure: Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. App Permissions

To provide full functionality, MemoFlow may request the following permissions upon your explicit action:

  • Storage / Filesystem (Optional): Used exclusively to export your notes into HTML, Markdown, or JSON files and save them to your device, or to import existing backup files.
  • Identity / Accounts (Optional): Used strictly for Google Sign-In when you choose to enable the Google Drive synchronization feature.

4. Third-Party Services (Ad-Free & Tracker-Free)

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade your data with any third parties. MemoFlow does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking tools, or analytics databases that monitor your activity. The only external service optionally utilized is Google Drive (governed by the Google Privacy Policy) when explicitly enabled by you for cloud synchronization.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Since your data is stored locally on your device, we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in your device settings will permanently erase all locally stored content immediately.
  • Google Drive Sync Data: Retained in your personal Google Drive until you delete it through the app's settings or revoke access via your Google Account Permissions.
  • Support Inquiries: If you contact us via email, your email address and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry (up to 1 year) and permanently deleted thereafter.

6. Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA & International Laws)

Regardless of your country of residence (including the EEA, UK, United States, South Korea, and other jurisdictions), you hold complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Right to Access, Portability, and Erasure: Because we do not maintain central user accounts on our servers, you can directly exercise your rights to access, export (via HTML, Markdown, or JSON), modify, or permanently delete your data within the Service at any time without contacting us.
  • CCPA / CPRA Notice (California Residents): We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Service is intended for a general audience and is safe for users of all ages. Because MemoFlow does not collect, store, or share any personal information on external servers and contains no advertisements, it is fully compliant with global children's privacy protection standards, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory changes. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at least 7 days prior to taking effect.
