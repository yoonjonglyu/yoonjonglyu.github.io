Global Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026 | Effective Date: October 26, 2026 (Previous: January 29, 2025)

This Global Privacy Policy explains how RyuisLabs ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, protects, and handles your information across our mobile applications, web services, and software tools (collectively, the "Services") available worldwide. We are committed to privacy-first engineering and full compliance with international data protection laws, including the GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA/CPRA (USA), COPPA, and PIPA (South Korea).

1. Information Collection and Purpose of Use

Many of our Services are engineered on a Local-First or Zero-Knowledge architecture, meaning your personal content resides primarily on your own device. Depending on the specific Service and features you choose to use, we process data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Local Application Data: Content you create (such as notes, logs, settings, or encrypted vaults) is stored locally on your device to provide core functionality offline.
  • User Authentication & Cloud Sync (Optional): When you explicitly choose to sign in via Google OAuth or enable cloud synchronization, we process your basic account identifiers (such as email address, profile name, or authentication token) and application data folders solely to authenticate your session and synchronize your data across devices.
  • On-Device Sensor & Camera Processing (Where Applicable): Certain Services may request camera or sensor permissions (e.g., for QR code scanning or on-device vision verification). All sensory feeds are processed strictly in real time within your device's volatile memory (RAM) and are never recorded, saved to external servers, or shared.
  • Service Improvement & Legal Compliance: To maintain system stability, respond to customer support inquiries, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

2. Google API Services & Limited Use Disclosure

When you choose to connect our Services with your Google Account, we may request access to specific Google API scopes based on your explicit consent:

  • Google Account Profile: Basic profile information (email address, display name, profile picture) used strictly for user authentication.
  • Google Drive / Calendar API Data (Optional): Access is restricted to isolated application data folders (appDataFolder) or specific resources you explicitly authorize to back up, synchronize, or manage your workflow. We do not access, view, or modify your unrelated personal files.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: RyuisLabs' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never use Google user data for serving advertisements or training external AI models.

3. Third-Party Services, Advertising & International Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. However, certain Services may integrate trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as Google LLC), which may process technical data on global servers (including in the United States):

  • Advertising (Free-Tier Apps with Google AdMob): In Services supported by advertisements, Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), coarse IP address, device specifications, and ad interaction logs to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads / iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Payments (Paid Apps & In-App Purchases): All financial transactions are processed directly by official marketplace billing systems (such as Google Play Billing). We never collect, access, or store your credit card numbers or banking details.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information only if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy:

  • Local Device Data: Retained on your device until you clear the app's storage, reset the in-app data, or uninstall the application.
  • Cloud Sync & Authenticated Data: Retained in your linked cloud storage until you delete the data within the Service or revoke access via your Google Account Permissions. Upon explicit deletion request, any server-side session data is erased immediately.
  • Customer Support Records: Email inquiries are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request (up to 1 year) and permanently deleted thereafter.

5. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit and at Rest: All network communications (including Google API transmissions) utilize HTTPS/TLS encryption. Sensitive local vaults employ authenticated cryptographic standards (such as AES-256-GCM).
  • Least-Privilege Access: Our applications request only the minimum device permissions and API scopes necessary to deliver the features you explicitly invoke.

6. Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPA & International Laws)

Regardless of your location worldwide, you have the right to:

  • Access, Rectify, Export, or Erase Data: Request access to, correction of, portability of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Restrict Processing & Withdraw Consent: Revoke API permissions, disable cloud synchronization, or reset advertising identifiers at any time via your device or account settings.
  • CCPA/CPRA Non-Discrimination: California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected, opt out of third-party ad sharing, and not be discriminated against for exercising their privacy rights.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 14 or 16). If we become aware that personal data from a child has been inadvertently submitted to us without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our Services or global legal requirements. Any material changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date at least 7 days prior to taking effect.
