# 🌐 RyuisLabs: Deep-Tech Indie Studio Web Platform

> *"Behind every achievement lies an unrelenting, deliberate process."*  
> *"We do not rely on chance. Business success and system operations are the deliberate results of engineered causality."*

**RyuisLabs** is the official engineering web platform dedicated to articulating the technical architecture, business tiers, and product portfolio of RyuisLabs—including the flagship **`RYUis : STATUS`** on-device AI vision verification engine, the ABILITY productivity series, MemoFlow, and SeedVault.

Engineered to discard the transient feel of generic developer portfolios, the platform evokes the gravitas and procedural legitimacy of architectural blueprints.

As an indie deep-tech studio, RyuisLabs pioneers **"technologies that substantiate human focus and physical mastery into verifiable data."**

## Core Flagship & Product Ecosystem

### 1. Flagship Engine: `RYUis : STATUS`
A privacy-preserving status interface that verifies focus and physical exercise through on-device AI vision:
- **MODULE_01 / FOCUS_VISION**: Eye-tracking pipeline determining Pure Focus Time without video retention.
- **MODULE_02 / PHYSICAL_VISION**: Pose estimation tracking joint flexion angles and repetition fidelity.
- **MODULE_03 / ZERO_KNOWLEDGE_LOG**: Raw video streams are purged immediately post-inference; only cryptographic statistical vectors are retained.
- **Commercial Tiers**: B2C (Personal training logs), B2B2C (Third-party verification reports for tutors/coaches), B2B (Licensing verification SDK/APIs for study spaces and gym facilities).

### 2. Product Showcase
- **ABILITY Series**:
  - `ABILITY: Gravity & Time`: Sci-fi themed focus timer utilizing Wakelock and native alarm precision.
  - `ABILITY: DaoXin`: Eastern cultivation habit tracker powered by React, Jotai, and pure vanilla CSS.
- **Standalone Utilities**:
  - `MemoFlow`: Volatile, offline-first PWA planner.
  - `SeedVault`: Military-grade local zero-knowledge vault utilizing Argon2id and AES-256-GCM.

## Frontend Architecture Highlights

### 1. Subpath-Based Static Routing i18n (`/[lang]/...`)
- Employs Next.js 16 App Router `generateStaticParams()` to pre-render all 34 localized routes statically at build time.
- Edge middleware dynamically detects user language preferences via `Accept-Language` headers and routes to localized canonical subpaths.

### 2. Zero-Overhead Styling
- Harmonies between Tailwind CSS 4 and scoped `*.module.css` modules prevent global cascade contamination while delivering 60fps rendering speeds.
- Designed with high-density mobile breakpoints (`420px`) ensuring engineering blueprints scale cleanly down to mobile screens.

### 3. Motion & Accessibility
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` to bypass terminal boot animations for users preferring reduced sensory input.
- Lightweight `IntersectionObserver` reveal primitives providing smooth, performance-neutral page transitions.
